The Challenge

The Competency Assessment workflow involved collecting self-assessments and supervisor evaluations across numerous Bureaus, Services, Offices, and Regional Offices. Preparing individual assessment results from Microsoft Forms responses required repetitive spreadsheet processing, scoring, formatting, and verification.

As the work progressed, additional challenges emerged: inconsistent employee names, differing file layouts, incomplete validation information, manually assigned intervention priorities, and the need to consolidate results from many individual assessment workbooks.

In parallel, Learning & Development reporting required cleaning large volumes of learner-response data, reconciling inconsistent Udemy course titles, and translating quantitative and qualitative findings into useful dashboards and reports.

Workstream 01: Competency Assessment Automation

My first major contribution began as a web-based assessment-status monitoring tool. After presenting a working version to the team, I was asked to expand it into a system capable of generating the actual Competency Assessment Results.

01. CA Results Generator

An application that evolved from monitoring assessment completion to producing structured employee competency reports.

  • Imported and consolidated raw assessment records from Excel exports using SheetJS.
  • Tracked self-assessment and supervisor-evaluation completion through a searchable employee dashboard.
  • Applied employee-name normalization, record deduplication, and profile-merging logic to handle variations in submitted records.
  • Used a competency reference dictionary to connect survey responses with official competency categories, descriptions, and levels.
  • Calculated weighted assessment scores using 40% self-assessment and 60% supervisor evaluation, alongside competency-gap computations.
  • Generated formatted Excel CA Results with ExcelJS, including structured cells, headings, and report formatting.
  • Underwent several revisions to improve naming conventions, competency-level mapping, and consistency with the required report template.

02. PDF & Signing Document Utilities

Supporting tools developed for converting assessment workbooks and preparing formal documents for employee and supervisor review.

  • Created a separate batch-conversion workflow for producing PDF versions of CA Results.
  • Developed a browser-based PDF generator for assessment summaries requiring employee and supervisor signature sections.
  • Extracted employee information, competency results, and intervention details from structured workbooks.
  • Used jsPDF and AutoTable for report layout, tables, page handling, and signatures.
  • Integrated employee reference data to improve name and position-title consistency.
  • Used JSZip for packaging batch-generated documents into downloadable archives.

03. Interventions Consolidator

Developed to transform individual validated assessment results into consolidated information for Learning & Development planning.

  • Parsed batches of completed CA Result workbooks and extracted competency gaps, recommended interventions, and priority rankings.
  • Standardized office identifiers and intervention categories for reporting.
  • Generated organizational summaries covering competency needs, interventions, and priority distributions.
  • Produced multi-sheet Excel reports and an interactive HTML analytics dashboard.
  • Supported exceptional cases, including competencies with zero identified gaps that nevertheless received interventions.
  • Underwent iterative revisions following discussions with the Monitoring & Evaluation and Learning & Development teams.

04. Individual Development Plan Generator

A bulk document-generation utility designed to prepare individualized development outputs from completed Competency Assessment records.

  • Processed assessment files uploaded from folder-based collections.
  • Retrieved employee and competency information using supporting reference datasets.
  • Cleaned extracted text and standardized employee names and position titles.
  • Generated individual PDF documents and accompanying Excel outputs where applicable.
  • Packaged generated documents using JSZip while preserving available folder organization.
  • Included progress indicators and handling for source files that did not match the expected layout.

Workflow Automation with Microsoft 365

Beyond the browser-based utilities, I developed TypeScript Office Scripts to help update existing Competency Assessment files without having to regenerate every report.

These scripts handled tasks such as protecting structural columns, inserting intervention dropdowns, adding priority-ranking fields, and extracting priority information from validation remarks using regular expressions.

I also created and tested Power Automate flows to apply scripts across large collections of assessment workbooks. During implementation, I addressed issues involving folder filtering, validation ranges, and differences in file structure. The workflows were reviewed and used collaboratively by the team.

Workstream 02: Udemy Data Engineering & BI

My Learning & Development work included two related analytical activities: evaluating the Batch 2025 Udemy Learners' Experience Survey and preparing a separate post-training evaluation dataset for dashboard reporting.

These activities involved different datasets and reporting requirements. I developed supporting tools to reduce repetitive data preparation and improve the consistency of the resulting analysis.

05. Udemy Course Masterlist Generator

A browser-based utility for creating a standardized reference list of course titles from curated Excel workbooks.

  • Scanned multiple uploaded files and worksheets for course-title entries.
  • Filtered irrelevant values and standardized equivalent text forms.
  • Deduplicated titles, including variations involving ampersands and the word "and."
  • Exported a sorted, consolidated Excel master list for subsequent validation and data-cleaning work.

06. Panglinis — Udemy Data Cleaner

A dedicated browser-based data-cleaning utility developed to reconcile inconsistent course titles in more than 12,000 learner-response rows.

  • Accepted raw Excel data and an official course-title master list.
  • Normalized strings by handling punctuation, spacing, and selected irrelevant words.
  • Applied custom mapping rules and keyword overlap to identify likely official titles.
  • Used character-bigram Sørensen–Dice similarity for approximate text matching, including spelling variations.
  • Recovered possible course titles from associated links when title-based matching was insufficient.
  • Separated matched and unmatched records for review and exported the resulting cleaned workbook.
  • Complemented manual checking rather than assuming that every raw entry could be reliably corrected automatically.

07. Udemy Power BI Dashboards & Reports

Developed interactive reports and analytical materials to evaluate learner experiences, satisfaction, course quality, and Learning & Development implementation.

  • Cleaned and transformed survey data using Power Query, including unpivoting, conditional columns, and score aggregation.
  • Created interactive report pages using charts, KPI cards, slicers, and customized page navigation.
  • Analyzed the Batch 2025 Udemy Learners' Experience Survey, which recorded 245 respondents and an overall reported satisfaction score of 4.65 out of 5.
  • Combined quantitative survey results with qualitative feedback on accessibility, learning content, workload constraints, and program administration.
  • Prepared a separate 17-page written analytical report presenting findings and recommendations.
  • Produced additional reporting work involving course-level satisfaction information and the implementation of the "Drop Everything and Learn" (DEAL) study period.

Additional Analytical & Reporting Work

Alongside the main applications, I developed supporting data-processing and reporting utilities to respond to specific operational requirements.

  • Employee Master List Generator: Converted structured employee rosters into JavaScript reference data for standardized name and position lookup.
  • Competency Ranking Comparator: Developed an analytical utility comparing simple percentage rankings, Bayesian averages, and Wilson score methods.
  • Training Evaluation Documentation: Prepared post-training documentation, resource-speaker evaluation reports, and supporting analytical summaries for Learning & Development activities.
  • Validation Coordination: Assisted in monitoring assessment completion, checking report readiness, and coordinating corrections with relevant teams and offices.

The Outcome

The work introduced reusable applications and automation scripts into processes that had previously depended on repetitive spreadsheet handling. The CA Results Generator was adopted by the team for report preparation, while supporting PDF, consolidation, and document utilities addressed later stages of the assessment workflow.

Power Automate and Office Scripts helped streamline updates to large collections of existing workbooks. Meanwhile, data-cleaning utilities and Power BI reporting supported more consistent Learning & Development analysis and review.

Beyond the technical deliverables, the experience strengthened my ability to translate operational requirements into working software, respond to stakeholder feedback, troubleshoot data-quality issues, and communicate analytical results in an organizational setting.
